A Peel For Help:
Vee DiMinci asks you to visit the local dog trainer
The local dog trainer wants 500 gold and 20 rat meat in exchange
Return to Vee: he agrees, but doesn't have the rat meat.
You return to the dog trainer with the rat meat.
You return to Vee, he asks you to clear out the orchard.
You fight 8 packs of rats and Scurvius
Return to Vee who awards you with gold and a staff that allows anyone to cast white magic.

Dig, Dig a Tunnel:
Meet Jenny outside a tunnel, asks if you're there to help and you say yes.
Cutscene of the people actually meant to help coming up and finding out, confronting you in the tunnel.
The Captain lets you stay if you make yourself useful.
Cast heal on 6 tunnelers.
Cast Ground Strike on 10 boulders.
Move three piles of sand out of the way
Cutscene with Harry, Jenny, and Kylie retreating from hyenas.
Defeat 3 packs of hyenas.
Defeat Alpha Hyena.
Quest complete (reward TBD).

Dryadic Ritual:
You go into the castle basement.
Find the 4 hints to unlock the gate (can be done without if you know the code with a short cutscene)
Fight and defeat the Dryad
Receive a piece of gear of your choice, 500 gold, and the ability to teleport back.

Pet Cemetery:
Little Tiffany learns you're a necromancer, asks you to try and bring her dog back to life.
Cut to Kelly watching the scene, retuning the dog to life as a demon.
You succeed, but Little Tiffany's dog is a Hellhound that tries to kill her and your party.
You defeat the dog, and Little Tiffany has to come to terms with death and that his dog is gone forever.
